Water loop. Feed water (a waste-water stream carrying the nutrients) enters by cam-lock at the rear, through a backflow preventer, strainer, flow meter and float-controlled solenoid into the return tank. The pump sends water through a filter to the supply main, forward to the nose risers, and into an inlet spreader in each run. Water flows front to rear under the mat, spills over the rear weirs, passes the debris screen and returns to the tank. Nitrate, EC, pH, DO and temperature are monitored only; when nutrients run low the 3-way valve sends water out the rear discharge.
Sixteen 3'-0" runs cannot sit side by side in an 8'-1½" trailer, so they are stacked 2 across × 8 high. Water alone is about 30,000 lb. Check axle loads and the reefer floor rating before you size the frame.
Off-the-shelf products that fit each role, everything except the duckweed run trays. Models are representative: confirm size, rating, wet-location suitability and lead time with the supplier before ordering.
